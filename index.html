How I work

Direction is a system of decisions.

The portfolio is organized around three capabilities: defining the vision, enabling the team and protecting the result through production.

01

Define

Establish the visual thesis, world rules, references, design language and quality bar that give the project a distinctive identity.

02

Align

Translate creative ambition into shared priorities, clear feedback, production documents and decisions teams can act on.

03

Deliver

Maintain visual coherence through reviews, collaboration and hands-on intervention from early exploration to final production.

Profile

Artist by craft. Director by responsibility.

I have spent more than twenty-five years creating and directing visual work across games, animation, publishing and original IP. My role is to turn an ambition into a world teams can understand, build and protect—combining hands-on visual development with leadership, production awareness and clear creative communication.

Based in Huế, Vietnam, I collaborate remotely with studios, founders and international teams, with travel when the project requires it.
