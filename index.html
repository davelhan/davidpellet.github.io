Approach

Define. Align. Deliver.

Art direction is a production system: a clear visual promise, shared language and decisions that survive contact with schedules, technology and multidisciplinary teams.

01

Define

Establish the visual thesis, emotional promise, references, quality bar and boundaries that make a world recognizable.

02

Align

Translate the same vision for concept, environment, characters, lighting, VFX, materials, tech art, design and production.

03

Deliver

Review, prioritize and iterate with teams until the final experience retains the original intent under production constraints.
