The mandate

Own the visual identity from concept to campaign.

My responsibility was not limited to environment supervision or final visual approval. I owned the artistic direction of the expansion as a whole: defining the identity of Ireland, translating that identity into executable direction for each department, directing the art team daily on the production floor, aligning priorities with production, and protecting the vision through final delivery.

The central challenge was to create a territory that felt immediately new and memorable while remaining fully credible inside Assassin’s Creed Valhalla. Every discipline had to contribute to the same world: landscape, architecture, materials, characters, lighting, atmosphere, VFX, technical solutions and marketing representation.

Confidentiality

Internal art-direction decks, benchmarks, review packs, production notes, paintovers and iteration documents remain confidential and are not displayed. This page describes their function, the leadership process and the public result without reproducing proprietary material.

Methodology

Direction as a production system.

My methodology combined a clear visual thesis, department-specific documentation, direct floor leadership and continuous validation. The aim was to make the direction memorable at the highest level and actionable at the level of everyday production decisions.

01

Define the visual thesis

Establish the emotional promise, visual identity, quality bar and creative boundaries before individual assets begin to diverge.

02

Build a shared source of truth

Create structured direction documents that turn broad ambition into references, rules, priorities, comparisons and decision criteria.

03

Translate by discipline

Reframe the same vision for concept art, level art, lighting, terrain, technical art, VFX, materials and character production.

04

Direct on the floor

Review work directly with artists, resolve ambiguity quickly, give visual feedback and keep creative decisions connected to production reality.

05

Align with production

Work closely with production to prioritize effort, manage dependencies, protect quality and keep the artistic ambition achievable.

06

Protect continuity to market

Carry the product’s visual identity into the final promotional campaign so the public image represents the game accurately and powerfully.

Marketing Art Direction

Extending the game’s identity into a global campaign.

I completely directed the final marketing visuals in direct collaboration with the Ubisoft Montreal team. My role was to ensure that the campaign did not become a generic fantasy image, but remained a concentrated, immediately readable expression of the expansion’s actual visual identity.

I guided the composition, character staging, environment, monumentality, atmosphere, lighting, narrative clarity, visual hierarchy and the controlled suggestion of the supernatural. I then supervised the adaptation of the final composition across landscape, square and vertical formats.

PlaceMake Ireland visually present through landscape, vegetation, moisture, scale and color.
ThreatCommunicate the Children of Danu as a ritual and cultural danger, not generic enemies.
MonumentalityUse ancient structures and environmental scale to imply history, mystery and weight.
RestraintTease the fantastic without explaining or revealing everything in a single image.
